iOS / iPadOS: do it manually
  1. You must be in Safari (Chrome and Firefox on iOS cannot add to the Home Screen).
  2. Tap the Share button in Safari's own toolbar — the square with the up arrow, not the button on this page.
  3. Scroll the sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then open the new icon. The green banner at the top of this page confirms it launched standalone.
